3. SECURITY

Preserve and strengthen the existing security architecture.

There are three database roles:

* Owner
* Coach
* Client

Verify that:

* Clients cannot promote themselves.
* Coaches cannot promote themselves or other users.
* Only Owners can change roles.
* Only authorized Owners can assign clients to coaches.
* Coaches can only access assigned clients.
* Clients can only access their own private data.
* Owners retain intended administrative access.
* role cannot be changed through normal client profile updates.
* coach_id cannot be manipulated by unauthorized users.
* No private client data is publicly accessible.
* No service-role/private key exists in frontend code.
* RLS remains enabled.
* SECURITY DEFINER functions use safe search paths.
* Privileged RPCs are properly restricted.

Do not weaken existing RLS.
