Title: How to delete your MyFitnessPal account – MyFitnessPal Help
Fetched: 2026-09-02T13:00:21.924Z

[Skip to main content](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032622451-How-to-delete-your-MyFitnessPal-account#main-content)

This article explains how to delete your MyFitnessPal account on iOS, Android, and the website. It also notes that account deletion is permanent and can't be undone, and points you to support if you need further help.

If needed, you can delete your account on our iOS and Android apps, and on our website with the steps below.

Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. It may take our outbound email system up to 72 hours to remove you from our email rotation.

* * *

### **iOS**

1. Open the MyFitnessPal app
2. Tap on the **More** menu in the bottom right. If you don’t have a More menu in your app, tap your **Profile Icon** in the upper right instead.
3. Scroll down and tap **Help**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.


* * *

### **Android**

1. Open the MyFitnessPal app
2. Tap the **More** menu in the bottom right of your screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Help**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.

* * *

### **Web**

1. Log into our [website](https://www.myfitnesspal.com/account/login) using the same email address and password that you use with the app.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Delete Account**.

* * *

If you’re having trouble accessing your account, our help article on [how to retrieve a forgotten password or username](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032273512-How-to-retrieve-a-forgotten-password-or-username) may help.

If you’re experiencing another issue accessing your account, or if you need more help, please [submit a support request](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) to our customer happiness team for further assistance.
