# Features

[Hevy](https://www.hevyapp.com/) offers a wide range of features while retaining its core simplicity and focusing on three pillars: **workout logging**, **progress tracking**, and **socializing**. Browse through the list of features below and see how they can help you stay motivated and reach your fitness goals.

_As Hevy grows, we’ll continue to expand this page, so check back from time to time to stay up to date._
