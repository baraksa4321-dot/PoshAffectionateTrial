Check-in forms are an easy way for you to stay connected with your clients and track their ongoing progress. Your clients can fill them out regularly to share how they’re feeling, what they’ve been working on, and their progress toward goals.

Customize your client's experience and collect information on multiple areas of health and wellness by using multiple forms!

**Here is what a check-in form typically covers:**

- **Progress Updates**: How are workouts, nutrition, and/or habits going? (Possibly on a scale of 1-10)
- **Well-being Check**: How is the client feeling physically (energy, soreness) or mentally (motivation, stress)?
- **Goals & Adjustments**: Are they on track or do they need adjustments to stay aligned with their goals?
- **Challenges**: What obstacles are they facing, whether in workouts, nutrition or consistency?
- **Feedback**: Are they enjoying their program or are there areas for improvement?
- **End of training survey:** Have a client that's finished their programming? Are they possibly moving on to other things– get feedback on their experience training with you!

## How to Create a Check-in Form

Check-in Forms can only be created by the **Account Owner or Admins**. _Managers, shared trainers, and regular trainers cannot create forms._

1\. Hover over the **Master Libraries** tab and select **Forms** from the menu options. This will take you directly to the form builder.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/44008682077716)

2\. On the Forms tab, click the blue **NEW** button to create a new form.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/44013535400724)

3\. Drag and drop the items you would like to include into your form from the menu options:

- Single Answer Question, Multiple Answer Question, Text Answer Question, A Rating from 1-10.



![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712098144660)

4\. Once you have dragged and dropped a question, enter the relevant information into the available fields:


  - Select the blue **Add an option** text to add answer under your question when applicable.
  - The maximum number of answers you can include per single/multiple type questions is 7.
  - Toggle the **Required Switch** to the **ON** or **OFF** positon to make a question optional/required

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712048751892)

5\. Repeat these steps until you have all the questions / components that you would like included in your Check-in Form.

6\. Once your form is complete, hit the blue **SAVE** button.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712098152340)

To preview your form as it will appear to a client, select the **PREVIEW** button.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712048762004)

## How to Edit a Check-in Form

Check-in Forms can only be edited by the **Account Owner or Admins**. _Managers, shared trainers, and regular trainers cannot edit forms._

Once your check-in form is created, it cannot be deleted. If you do not wish for your clients to receive the form(s), simply disable the feature in the settings.

1\. From the overview page, hover over the **Master Libraries** tab and select **Forms** from the menu options.

2\. Click the **Edit** button next to the Check-in Form you would like to adjust.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712048765844)

3\. Modify the information / questions on your check in form:

- Delete a question by clicking the **bin icon:**

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712048773908)

- **Drag and drop** to reorder questions:

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712048778900)

- **Edit content** by clicking on the related text-boxes/fields:

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712098173332)

- Click the **Paper icon** to duplicate an existing question.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/44008703062420)

4\. To save your edits, click the blue **SAVE** button.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712048792468)

Please note: If the Check-in Form is already scheduled on a Master Program or a client’s calendar, updates won’t apply automatically. You’ll need to re-schedule the form for the changes to take effect.

## How to Schedule Check-in Forms Directly into a Program Calendar

1\. From the Overview page, navigate to the **Master Programs** tab.

2\. Navigate to your Master Program calendar and select the blue **Schedule** button. ChooseCheck-in Form from the drop-down menu.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712962587412)

3\. You have the option to schedule one or multiple forms on a specific day and/or set-up repeating options for recurring check-ins.

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/44008682083092)

4\. Once finished, hit the blue **SAVE** button.

## How Clients Receive a Check-in Form

Once your check-in form has been assigned to a client or scheduled into a program, **clients will receive a push notification** **when it's time** to fill out their check-in form:

![](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/41712962591252)

If a client closes the notification, they can access their form via their client dashboard once they're ready to fill it out.

Clients can enable this 'Daily Reminder' notification within their [notification settings](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004707626) on the mobile app. This will ensure they receive all items scheduled in the calendar, including the check-in form if it is scheduled.

Clients will see the Check-in form as a scheduled item in their **THINGS TO DO TODAY** section on the client dashboard (on mobile and web):

![Screenshot 2025-11-18 at 12.08.44 PM.png](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/43684960126996)

## Check-In Form Notifications

Trainers can receive in-app notifications when a client submits a Check-in Form. This helps you stay up to date with client responses without needing to manually check for new submissions.

When a client submits a Check-in Form:

- On web, a notification appears in the notification center, accessible from the bell icon.
- On mobile, an in-app notification is delivered to the coach’s mobile app.

Selecting the notification takes you directly to the submitted form so you can review the client’s response more quickly.

## Managing Check-In Form Notifications

To update your Check-in Form submission notifications, go to your account notification settings and adjust the **Custom Form Submissions** notification option. By default, the notifications are set to In-App. Email notifications for check-in forms are not currently supported.

![Screenshot 2026-05-11 at 2.08.41 PM.png](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/49140595833364)

For full steps on managing notification preferences, visit: [Managing General Notification Settings](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/en-us/articles/208688826).

## Edit a Submitted Check-In Form Response

Coaches with access to a client’s profile can edit answers on a previously submitted Check-In Form from the web app. This allows you to correct an answer or update information after following up with the client without asking them to complete the form again.

To edit a submitted Check-In Form:

1. Open the client’s profile card.
2. Navigate to the **Forms** tab.
3. Open the submitted Check-In Form you want to update.
4. Select the **Edit** icon.

![Screenshot 2026-08-31 at 1.21.27 PM.png](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/53100618155540)
5. Update the client’s answers as needed.
6. Select **Submit** at the bottom of the form.

![Screenshot 2026-08-31 at 1.23.57 PM.png](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/53100618156692)

Required questions cannot be left blank when saving your changes.

After the form is updated, the most recent editor and edit date are shown on the submitted form. Any form comparison views will also use the updated answers.

![Screenshot 2026-08-31 at 1.22.59 PM.png](https://help.trainerize.com/hc/article_attachments/53100625090964)

## FAQ

**How many forms can I create?**

Account Owners and Admins can create an unlimited number of forms under the **Master Libraries > Forms** section of their web account.

**Why can't my trainer create a Check-in Form?**

_Only Account Owners and Admins_ can create and/or edit Check-in Forms.

Managers, Shared Trainers, and Regular Trainers cannot create them, but can schedule existing Check-in Forms onto client program calendars.

**Can trainers receive notifications when a client submits a Check-in Form?**

Yes. Trainers can receive in-app notifications when a client submits a Check-in Form.

These notifications can be managed from **My Account > Notifications > Custom Form Submissions**. You can choose whether to receive in-app notifications or turn them off.
