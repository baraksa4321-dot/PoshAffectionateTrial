Since we first launched in 2008, we’ve been dedicated to one mission: [making weight loss less difficult](https://www.loseit.com/articles/advantages-of-switching-to-lose-it-app/) by building the best tools for the device in your pocket. Now, we’re thrilled to unveil a total redesign of our app. As the biggest evolution of our app in over a decade, this isn’t just a new look. It’s a reimagining of how [Lose It!](https://loseit.sng.link/Ads36/i3kt/dqng) should feel in 2026: faster, clearer, and more supportive of your daily goals.

## Why We Redesigned

For years, our priority has been [building a powerful tool](https://www.loseit.com/articles/5-lose-it-features-to-make-logging-easier/) — [expanding features](https://www.loseit.com/articles/how-do-i-use-lose-it-s-new-snap-it-feature/) and constantly improving the engine under the hood. However, this focus led to an important trade-off: While the [app was becoming more capable](https://www.loseit.com/articles/you-ve-never-logged-food-like-this-before/), its top-level appearance began to feel like a reflection of an earlier era. To remain a premier experience for our members, our design needed to be as modern and sophisticated as the technology powering it.

We stepped back and asked a simple but powerful question:

_If we were building Lose It! for the first time today, what would that experience feel like?_

This redesign is our answer.

We shifted our focus to the core elements of the app to ensure that our interface reflects the high-quality, modern product we are proud to support.

## Preparing for the New Experience

Before we could apply a fresh coat of paint to the interface, we had to confront a bigger truth: Many of our core features were originally built for a very different era of mobile. Early “mobile best practices” prioritized information density, cramming as much as possible into small screens with limited space. Today’s devices are larger, more fluid, and built for motion, flow, and clarity.

To meet that shift, we spent months rethinking some of our oldest and most foundational features.

- **[Food Search](https://www.loseit.com/articles/a-smarter-simpler-food-search-is-here/):** We rewrote our most complex feature from scratch to prioritize clarity and ensure our new design language could handle our most detail-oriented tools.
- **The Log Header:** We replaced the rigid, pinned navigation bar with a cleaner, free-floating summary that moves naturally with the rest of the page.
- **Contextual Logging:** We freed the tab bar by repositioning the “Plus” and “Done Logging” buttons to more intuitive, reachable places, right where they’re most useful.
- **Modernized Code:** We rewrote significant portions of the app’s foundation to make the experience faster for members and easier for our team to maintain.

For our users, this meant that many of the oldest, most central parts of the app evolved long before this visual launch. This redesign builds on that groundwork.

## Evolution, Not Redefinition

Our goal for the redesign was to modernize the experience without changing our personality, so one of the first key steps was to spend time listening to our community to understand what makes Lose It! feel like Lose It!

The answers were consistent.

[Lose It! should feel warm and welcoming](https://www.loseit.com/articles/secrets-tips-and-tricks-for-using-lose-it/). It’s a space where progress is encouraged and data is presented with no judgment. Oh, and also: confidently orange.

So we listened, and went all in on our signature color, featuring a new primary orange that offers better contrast and readability while maintaining the warmth you expect. We explored hundreds of iterations before landing on our new background — a fun, energetic nod to our orange top bar of the past that feels perfectly at home in this new era.

## Reimagined Themes

Weight loss is deeply personal, and in 2026, users expect experiences that feel adaptable, expressive, and personal, not one-size-fits-all.

That’s why we completely reimagined Themes, a long-standing [Premium feature](https://www.loseit.com/articles/reasons-to-upgrade-to-lose-it-premium-in-the-new-year/) that lets members personalize the app based on their interests, moods, colors, and seasons.

Themes are now stronger and more expressive than ever. You can choose a vibe that matches your mood or motivation by changing the background on your main tabs and seeing curated colors throughout nearly every page of the app. Whether it’s Space, Dogs, or any of our more than 30 options, every theme has been thoughtfully rebuilt to feel intentional, readable, and consistent.

## Looking Forward

This redesign represents far more than a visual glow-up. It’s a new foundation for the future of Lose It!

By pairing beautiful design updates with massive under-the-hood rewrites in modern code, we’ve created an experience that’s faster, more reliable, and built to evolve with you. It honors our long-standing mission while setting a new standard for what Lose It! can be. This is just the beginning of our next chapter, and we’re excited to build it together.
