# Source: MyFitnessPal features

URL: https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032625951-MyFitnessPal-Premium-features

[Skip to main content](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032625951-MyFitnessPal-Premium-features#main-content)

This article explains what's included with a MyFitnessPal Premium subscription, including faster food logging, more control over goals, and deeper nutrition insights. It also covers how Premium works across devices and how it differs from Premium+.

A [Premium subscription](https://www.myfitnesspal.com/premium#features) adds new options to MyFitnessPal's robust feature set, increasing the app's customizability and flexibility. Everything you've accumulated in your MyFitnessPal account will stay just as you've built it: friends, favorite foods, diary history, but many new features are added to the mix, and some existing features get additional enhancements.

Premium is available on our website and in the latest versions of our iOS and Android apps. Your subscription provides you with Premium features across all three platforms.

* * *

### **Faster ways to log food**

[**Barcode Scanner**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032624771-How-do-I-use-the-barcode-scanner-to-log-foods)

Quickly log food by scanning the barcode on the package.

[**Meal Scan**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045761612-Meal-Scan-FAQ)

Identify the food using your smartphone camera. Suggests verified foods from our vast food database. (Available only on iOS and Android for English users)

[**Voice Logging**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/30332897072269-Voice-Logging)

A convenient and helpful way of logging food to your diary using your voice.

[**Multi-Day Logging**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/33535658002061-Multi-Day-Logging)

Add items to more than one day of the week in your diary.

[**Quick Add Macronutrients**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032621971-What-is-Quick-Add)

Quick Add users can now add macros to their calorie entries.

### **More control over your food**

[**Macronutrients by Gram**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032274432-Can-I-customize-my-nutritional-goals)

Set macronutrient goals by either gram or percentage.

[**Different Goals by Day**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032274432-Can-I-customize-my-nutritional-goals)

Set custom calorie and macronutrient goals for any day of the week.

[**Calorie Goals By Meal**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032273792-Calorie-Goals-By-Meal-FAQs)

Your calorie goals are now easier to reach.

[**Exercise Calorie Settings**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032623851-Why-do-my-daily-nutrient-values-and-my-calorie-goal-change-when-I-log-exercise)

Track exercises without increasing your calorie or nutrient goals.

[**Net Carbs**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032622531-Can-I-track-Net-Carbs)

Replace total carbs with net carbs for convenience tracking (Available only on iOS and Android for US and Canada)

[**Intermittent Fasting**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/10983207647117-Intermittent-Fasting-FAQ)

Track Daily fasting periods in real time from your diary, and follow your progress over time. (Available on iPhone and Android)

### **Deeper insights into your food**

[**Customize your Nutrient Dashboard**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032272532-Customize-your-Nutrient-Dashboard-with-MyFitnessPal-Premium)

Choose between a Macro-focused, Macros+Heart Healthy, or Macros+Carb Conscious view on your Today page.

[**Food Analysis**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032272712-Using-Food-Lists-with-MyFitnessPal-Premium)

[**Macros By Meal**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032625151-Macros-By-Meal-FAQs)

See a macronutrient breakdown for every meal you log right inside your diary.

[**Food Timestamps**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036224752-Food-Timestamps-FAQs)

Learn how the timing of your meals affects your energy, workout and more. (Available only on iOS and Android)

[**Unlimited Weekly Digests**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032622591-Weekly-Digest)

Revisit your nutrition and fitness progress for any week.

[**Data Export**](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032273352-Data-Export-FAQs)

Export your meal-level nutrition details, progress history, & exercise history.

### **Frequently asked questions**

**Do I need a separate subscription for each device?**

No. One Premium subscription covers the iOS and Android apps and the website. Sign in with the same account on each one.

**Why do I not see a Premium feature that is listed here?**

A few features are limited to certain devices, countries, or languages. Check the note next to the feature above. Updating to the latest version of the app also helps, since new features roll out gradually.

**Does upgrading change the data already in my account?**

No. Your friends, favorite foods, recipes, and diary history stay exactly as you built them. Premium adds features on top of what you already have.

**What is the difference between Premium and Premium+?**

Premium+ includes all features on this page plus the Meal Planner. Read the [full comparison of Free, Premium, and Premium+](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/34889191368077-What-s-the-difference-between-Free-Premium-and-Premium) to see which plan fits your goals.

**Why can I not open the Meal Planner with my Premium subscription?**

The Meal Planner is a Premium+ feature. Learn more about [what is included with Premium+](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/34347930588557-Premium) if you want to add it to your plan.

**How do I get Premium?**

Follow the steps to [upgrade to MyFitnessPal Premium](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032625771-How-do-I-upgrade-to-MyFitnessPal-Premium) on your device of choice.
