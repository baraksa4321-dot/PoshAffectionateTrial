## Frequently asked questions​​

What is the Habit tracking feature in My PT Hub?

The Habit tracker feature lets trainers assign, monitor and celebrate client lifestyle habits to support long-term behaviour change and improve overall progress beyond workouts and nutrition.

How do I assign habits to a client in My PT Hub?

You can assign habits from your dashboard by selecting the client, navigating to the Habits tab, and choosing pre-built templates or creating custom habits to assign. My PT Hub’s personal trainer software then tracks client’s logged habits automatically, allowing you to see their progress over time.

Can clients track habits on mobile and desktop?

Yes! Once assigned, clients can log habits daily via the mobile app or the desktop platform, with in-app prompts simplifying habit completion tracking.

Do clients receive notifications for habit reminders?

Clients with active habits receive automated reminders, including a morning prompt and an evening summary of their habit progress.

Can trainers create custom habits or use templates?

Absolutely. Trainers have the flexibility to choose from pre-built habit templates or create entirely unique habits tailored to each client’s goals.

How does logging work in the Habit tracker?

Clients can log habit completions directly from their dashboard or via the dedicated habits section in the app with a simple tap to mark completion.

Can I assign multiple habits to several clients at once?

Yes, habits can be assigned to individual or multiple clients simultaneously from the Habits area on either web or mobile.

Does the Habit tracker support accountability and long‒term habit building?

Yes, by tracking daily progress, reviewing completion rates and celebrating habit milestones, the Habit tracker tool helps reinforce accountability and habits that stick.
