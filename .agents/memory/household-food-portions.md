---
name: Household food portions
description: Approved policy for displaying and converting nutrition quantities across menu editing and food substitutions.
---

Use food-specific household units alongside grams: dense dairy and spreads can use tablespoons, teaspoons, and servings; liquids and oils can use milliliters, cups, tablespoons, and teaspoons; unit foods, slices, and measured staples should keep their natural units. Every displayed unit must convert through a reliable serving weight or volume before calculating macros. For common vegetables, the approved FoodsDictionary weights are: red pepper 110/185/289 g, green pepper 110/166/218 g, yellow pepper 86/122/188 g, cucumber 52/96/154 g, tomato 114/172/230 g, carrot 68/136/261 g, zucchini 108/218/290 g, and radish 18/32/49 g for small/medium/large.

**Why:** The user approved this policy because gram-only choices are not practical for everyday logging, but arbitrary household conversions would make nutrition values misleading. FoodsDictionary is the accepted source for the vegetable unit weights after the previous generic conversions materially understated large produce.

**How to apply:** Keep the conversion rules in the shared food-portion module and reuse them in coach menu editing, trainee logging, and replacement suggestions. Add a test whenever a new food category receives a household unit, and do not replace source-specific vegetable weights with a generic 100–200 g estimate.
