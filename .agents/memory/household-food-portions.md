---
name: Household food portions
description: Approved policy for displaying and converting nutrition quantities across menu editing and food substitutions.
---

Use food-specific household units alongside grams: dense dairy and spreads can use tablespoons, teaspoons, and servings; liquids and oils can use milliliters, cups, tablespoons, and teaspoons; unit foods, slices, and measured staples should keep their natural units. Every displayed unit must convert through a reliable serving weight or volume before calculating macros.

**Why:** The user approved this policy because gram-only choices are not practical for everyday logging, but arbitrary household conversions would make nutrition values misleading.

**How to apply:** Keep the conversion rules in the shared food-portion module and reuse them in coach menu editing, trainee logging, and replacement suggestions. Add a test whenever a new food category receives a household unit.
